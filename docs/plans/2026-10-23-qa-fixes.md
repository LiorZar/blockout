## My calls

1. **Chase on tracks** (`src/engine/chase-tracks.ts`, new; upstream's
   `chaseRoutine` keeps its signature and calls it, `overlayAt` goes): each performer has an arc distance along the serpentine as a
   function of time. The path extends straight back past its start, so pursuers
   start behind the runner at their own spots. Scripted beats ride the track: the
   vault, stumble and the pursuer's skid play their keyframe poses over the run
   loop while the root keeps going along the path (upstream already laid them
   with the run gait); the runner's skid brakes (to a quarter of its speed and
   back), and the pursuers, laid against the runner's track, brake with it. No
   travel sample falls inside a beat. A pursuer never comes within 0.9 m (along
   the path) of the one ahead before the tackle. Ending "caught": the pursuer's
   tackle closes from its gap to 0.45 m behind the runner at contact and stays
   there; the runner falls forward with its momentum (`trip-and-fall-flat`, a bot
   plays the library's trip-and-fall clip) instead of `knockdown-fall`, which
   throws a body backward into the tackler; both start 1.6 s from the end so the
   fall lands inside the shot. Ending "escape": the pursuer brakes to a stop and
   puts its hands on its hips. A bot's stumble keeps the keyframe pose over the
   run loop: the library's `trip` clip is a fall, not a stumble.
2. **Load race**: `stage_fight`, `update_fight`, `spawn_sequence`,
   `spawn_choreography`, `choreograph_entities` and `apply_motion` wait for the
   library when the request involves a bot (cast, fighters, bodies or entities),
   at most 120 s. On a timeout the tool fails with a clear message and stages
   nothing (an error, not a keyframe fight on the timeline). `get_state` reports
   `mocap: { ready, bots, clips }`. The Fight Director's own staging waits too
   (its notice already defers the solve).
3. **Bot footwork** (`src/engine/mocap/footwork.ts`, new): where a bot's marks
   carry its root further, or turn it more, than its clip's own root travel (a
   rest walk, a step-in, a stagger's step back, a push), the feet step instead of
   riding the root. As built (T3): the evaluator finds the windows itself from the
   track's legs (`clip-marks.ts`: runs of posed clip legs with travel past the
   clip, broken where the travel stops or turns back; each crossfade between two
   clips its own window; no new document field) and hands the pose the travel and
   turn done and in all (`ClipState.step`). The pose (`plantFeet`, at the end of
   `mocapClipPose`, so the engine and the renderer agree) holds each grounded foot
   and steps it in turn: the front foot first (a staggered fighter's front foot
   gets out of the blow's way), ≤ 0.4 m a step, lifted with the step (3.5–8 cm)
   while the other foot stands on the floor; two-bone leg IK with the knee in its
   own plane (`seat.ts`'s math), the hips sinking ≤ 8 cm where a step needs the
   reach. A foot off the floor (a kick), a body that isn't standing, a window
   through a fall (knockdown, KO, get-up) and an in-place travelling loop (on its
   warp) keep the clip's feet. Rest walks and step-ins between two clips become
   posed crossfades (no walk loop); a keyframe move steps in on the idle, then
   crossfades into its first pose where it stands. The stance swaps (crossfades
   to a mirrored block) step each foot from one clip's spot to the other's. The
   fight solve's own checks (separation, rest depth, weapon swings) read the bots
   without footwork (`ShotEvaluator` option `footwork: false`), so its marks keep
   HEAD's separation; the final contact measure and the timeline step.
4. **Cones leave the export**: the nightclub's beams are tagged editor-only and
   `renderFrameAt` hides them in every pass (they stay in the viewport while
   blocking). Two-line hook in `builders.ts` plus one in `SceneManager.ts`; a
   coloured translucent cone reads as a solid pyramid to a video model and draws
   solid in the depth pass.
5. **Prompt words**: the stunt and fall motions get verbs ("vaults", "stumbles",
   "skids to a stop", "is knocked down", "trips and falls flat", "tackles");
   pursuers who run behind the same runner are grouped ("COP 1 and COP 2 chase
   RUNNER …"), so every cast member is named in the action. The dropped beat is
   fixed by call 2; checked by re-running QA's race on the fixed app.
6. **`update_fight` length**: keep the fit rule (H3's 15 s cap); both tools warn
   when the fight runs past the shot ("The fight ends at 20.9 s, after the shot's
   18.5 s: set the shot to 21.5 s to see it all").
7. **BACKLOG**: QA's harness note (a long export over HTTP MCP outlives a 300 s
   client timeout; the app then refuses every edit until the export ends).
