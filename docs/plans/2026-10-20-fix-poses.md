# fix-poses: long guns carried low, Talk gestures at the chest

Lior's rulings (2026-10-20): a held rifle / shotgun at rest is carried muzzle-down across the body, about 40° down, grip hand at the hip / belt, support hand on the fore-end, so a walk reads as carrying (today it rests at the chest, 19° down, and reads as nearly aiming). The `talk` / `gesture` gait gestures at chest height instead of raising the hand overhead.

## My calls

- **Rest carry is a body-frame rule, blended with the shoulder rule by where the hand is.** The shoulder mount (stock points at the shoulder) can't put the grip at the hip *and* the muzzle 40° down: that line runs ~70° down. A long gun gains a `carry` (barrel pitch below level and yaw across the body, toward the off side, measured from the torso's level forward). `weaponRotation` turns from the shoulder rule to the carry as the shoulder→grip line drops from 45° to 60° below level: the aim, the recoil and `rifle-aim-fire`'s own low-ready keyframes (lines 12–35° down) keep the shoulder rule exactly, the idle carry (≥ 60°) is the pure carry. The torso orientation joins the hand frame (`HandFrame.torso`; `hold.ts` reads the rendered `torso` group), so FK and the rendered rig still agree.
- **The grip sits at the waist, not lower.** At 40° the fore-end is ~19 cm below the grip; the support arm reaches it only with the grip at about navel height (1.10 m on the 1.78 m man). Lower and the support hand slides off the fore-end.
- **No body retarget at rest.** The carry direction is the same on every body by construction; `longGunRetarget` (which matches the reference man's aim) scales with the shoulder-rule weight, so the aim keeps today's retarget and the carry gets none. The child still can't reach an adult rifle's fore-end; its slide is reported, as today.
- **`rifle-aim-fire` data is unchanged** (the brief): its low-ready keyframes stay at the chest; the motion starts from there.
- **Talk:** upper arm a little forward and turned in (shoulder `rotation.y`), forearm forward, hand in front of the chest below the shoulder; a small deterministic sway on `time` (forearm ±0.1 rad, turn ±0.08 rad). The stand-gait breathing bob (the feet) is untouched.
