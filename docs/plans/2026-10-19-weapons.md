# Weapons (held props)

Lior: "do we have weapon support (sword, guns, spears etc...) please add". Worker `weapons`, coordinator `crd`.

## Goal

A performer holds a weapon in either hand. The weapon follows that hand exactly through every motion, gait and fight, in the editor and in exports, deterministically. Sword, gun and spear fights stage in a click or two.

## Decisions

Fixed by crd: weapons are entities with `Entity.heldBy?: { entityId, hand: 'L' | 'R' }`. The engine owns the hand-frame math (`src/engine/weapons.ts`), and the renderer attaches each weapon to the rig's `elbowL` / `elbowR` group. The props are grey-box procedural, two-handed weapons attach to the main hand, and `prop.sword` keeps working.

My calls:
- **Hand frame.** Its origin is the hand point `(0, −lowerArmLen, 0)` in the elbow group, with the elbow group's axes. −Y runs along the forearm to the knuckles. −Z is the thumb side, which faces forward when the arm hangs. A fist's handle runs along Z.
- **Grip angle.** There is no wrist joint, so each weapon carries a fixed grip rotation. Blades and polearms sit about 69° from the thumb axis toward the forearm, so a thrust ends level, a slash follows through, and the blade points down at rest. Gun barrels run along the forearm.
- **Shared arm FK.** `skeleton.ts` exports positions only, so `weapons.ts` repeats its 4-link arm chain to get orientation. A test pins it to `poseEffectors` (1e-12) and to three.js (1e-6). If `skeleton.ts` later exports frames, switch to them.
- **Grip poses live in the engine.** `ShotEvaluator` blends an idle hold pose into the holder's joints, weighted by how idle that arm is: weight 1 with no arm channels, fading to 0 by 0.6 rad. Renderer and FK then agree, and exports get the pose.
- **Off-hand IK.** Two-handed weapons, except the bow, place the off hand on the weapon's support point with closed-form 2-bone IK (shoulder X/Z plus elbow). The bow's off hand draws the string, as the motion has it.
- **Spear and staff rest one-handed** (resumed session). At low ready the off hand can't reach the shaft in front of the grip, and the point behind it is a reach round the back, so at rest the off hand hangs free; it takes the shaft as the holding arm leaves its idle pose. Idle blades and shafts hold at low ready, tip forward and ~30° down (crd's look note).
- **Held weapons in the evaluator.** A held weapon's `EntityState` is its grip's world position and its holder's heading, so framing and `get_state` see where it is. This is exact for posed and stand gaits. Travelling gaits swing the arms in the renderer only.
- **Static weapons.** An unheld weapon is an ordinary static prop: melee weapons stand on their butt, guns lie flat, the shield stands on its rim. Dropping one leaves it standing at the hand's ground spot, facing the holder's heading.
- **Catalog.** There is a new `weapons` category with `prop.katana`, `prop.knife`, `prop.axe`, `prop.spear`, `prop.staff`, `prop.shield`, `prop.pistol`, `prop.rifle`, `prop.shotgun` and `prop.bow`. `prop.sword` and `prop.baseballBat` stay in Props and become holdable.
- **Default hands.** The bow and the shield go in L, everything else in R. A weapon held by its holder's same hand displaces the one already there, which drops.
- **Labels.** A spawned weapon gets the next free label colour and no text, so it reads as its own coloured proxy. The prompt names it: "a sword in A's right hand".
- **Gizmo.** A held weapon gets no transform gizmo, since it moves with the hand.
- **Weapon fights (phase B).** A blow from a hand holding a weapon lands with it: a blade 80 % of the way to its tip, a club 90 %, a point at the tip. A one-hand weapon move (sword slash / thrust, knife lunge) mirrors onto the weapon hand when only the other hand is armed, and a blow from a hand on a two-hander comes from its holding hand. An armed defender blocks and parries on its blade or shield (mid-blade, shield face), whichever hand holds it. An armed blow that can't reach by shoulder pitch also bends the elbow, and a high guard also swings the shoulder. A gun beat (`shoot` / `fire` = `pistol-aim-fire`, for every gun) fires on the aim keyframe just before the recoil: its contact point sits down the barrel line at about the target's distance, so the solver's closed-form placement puts the line through the target; the shooter holds its ground (no step-in, no re-closing), a block or parry on a bullet becomes a miss, and the hit reaction starts on the shot frame. Blows from weapons keep the hand's physics mass. All of it is measured through `ShotEvaluator` with the held weapons in the scene.
- **Motions.** Upstream motion data is never edited. Contradictions go to crd for BACKLOG: `rifle-aim-sweep` raises both forearms vertical, and the walk gait hyperextends the elbows.

## Tasks

1. **Engine core:** `weapons.ts` with the catalog, `handFrame`, `heldPointLocal`, `weaponTipWorld`, the mirror for L, idle poses and IK. Also the `assets.ts` entries and `types.ts` (`heldBy`, `weapons` category).
   Verify with `tests/unit/weapons.test.ts`: hand frame vs `poseEffectors` and vs three.js, tip directions for rest / thrust / slash / pistol aim, IK reaching its target, and catalog coverage.
2. **Schema:** `migrateProject` drops dangling or invalid `heldBy` and keeps only the first weapon per hand.
   Verify with a round-trip test and a dangling-link test.
3. **Evaluator hook:** `evaluate.ts` (an upstream file, small hook) calls `applyHolds` after the marriages.
   Verify with unit tests: a weapon's state equals its FK grip, a holder's joints get the grip pose, and the off hand is on the support point within 1 mm.
4. **Renderer:** `weapon-builders.ts` (new), a 1-line dispatch in `builders.ts`, and `hold.ts` (new, the hold matrix). In `SceneManager`, a held visual follows the elbow group each frame, the label sits above the grip, and a held weapon gets no gizmo.
   Verify with a unit test that the three.js weapon tip equals the engine tip (1e-6) over random poses, for every weapon and both hands.
5. **Store and UI:** `holdWeapon`, `spawnHeldWeapon` and `dropWeapon` in the store, with holds released when the holder is deleted. In the Inspector, a weapon shows "Held by / Hold in L/R / Drop" and a person shows "Arm with…". The Library gets a Weapons group and the prompt names held weapons.
   Verify with typecheck and lint, plus a store-level unit test where possible.
6. **MCP:** `hold_weapon`, `drop_weapon`, `list_weapons`, and `heldBy` in `get_state`, in `tools.json` and `handler.ts`.
   Verify with `mcp-catalog.test.ts`.
7. **e2e, smoke and visual:** in `tests/e2e/weapons.spec.ts`, `hold_weapon` a sword in R, play `sword-slash-combo`, and check that the rendered tip (`__blockout_scene` world matrices) matches the engine tip. Then export a clip. Smoke must be green, and a contact sheet goes to `.maestro/weapons-visual/sheet.png`.
8. **Phase B (after A is reported):** weapon fights. `A sword slash to head -> parry` puts the blade or tip on the target within 3 cm, measured through `ShotEvaluator`. Guns get a shot beat with an aim line. `fight/solve.ts` changes stay additive.
