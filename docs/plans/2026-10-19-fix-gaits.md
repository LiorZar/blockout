# fix-gaits: bodies touch the floor

Lane `fix-gaits`, round 3. Bugs from `.maestro/qa-cast/REPORT.md`: 4 (crouch sinks), 5 (sit soles), 6 (elderly lie), 1 (motions float).

## Goal

Every gait and every library motion keeps the body on the floor: the lowest point of the posed body is at y = 0 when it should touch, and nothing goes below it, on all six bodies (man, woman, child, elderly, both mannequins).

## My calls

- **One floor model for gaits and motions.** New engine module `src/engine/floor.ts`: a body proxy (balls and foot points on the FK bones from `skeleton.ts`) gives the lowest surface point of a posed body. Classic people use their exact capsules and boxes; mannequins use balls fitted to their section tables. A unit test checks the proxy against the rendered meshes over every library keyframe.
- **Gaits ground themselves.** `animatePerson` sets the root height from the proxy instead of fixed drops: crouch (balls of the feet on the floor), sit (hips at shin height, so thighs level, shins vertical, soles on the floor; the seat height now follows the body), lie / fall (lowest point on the floor; lie drops the elderly stoop, the body lies flat).
- **Walk keeps a foot on the floor; jog and run fly briefly.** Walk is stance + swing with the lowest foot pinned at every phase. Jog and run (crd follow-up) plant each foot for a contact length solved by leg IK, so the planted ankle never slides, then kick the heel up, drive the knee and fly with the feet at most 4 cm / 6.5 cm off the floor; run leans 10°, arms bent. The old run floated its feet 0.5 m in a split leap.
- **Motion floor rule** (`motionRootY`): a keyframe that doesn't lift the body (`move.up <= 0`) rests the body's lowest point on the floor (48 presets fold the legs with no drop); a lifted keyframe keeps its authored height unless a body part would go below the floor, which raises it just enough. The floor is the performer's base height (`pos.y`, 0 on the ground). Bodies are the performer's own (asset, params, scale), defaulting to the man.
- **Motion-playback marks carry `posedMove`** (not `lockFacing`, which the store uses to find fight marks). Only on a leg between two of them does the evaluator keep the facing and the `stand` gait and keep the body's lowest point on the line between keyframes (they used to play a walk cycle under the motion and turn toward vertical or backward moves). Travel legs, chase paths and formation walks stay upstream (crd ruling; tested against an upstream evaluation).
- Out of scope (BACKLOG via crd): push-ups, cartwheel, lift-and-carry (need a root pitch channel, bug 2).
